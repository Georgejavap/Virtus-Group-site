Welcome to Virtus Group Gym

Destination for all things fitness and wellness. At Virtus Group, we believe everyone has a fitness journey—either training for a sport or improving their image. Everyone has the right to develop their body and mind.

Our Gym


Weightlifting

Build Strength & Power

Our weightlifting zone is equipped with top-tier barbells, dumbbells, racks, and platforms designed for both beginners and serious lifters. Whether you're training for strength, size, or performance, we provide the tools and space to help you progress.

Olympic Platforms

Perfect for clean and jerks, snatches, and heavy deadlifts. Built with shock-absorbent flooring.

Free Weights

Over 3,000kg of free weights ranging from 2.5kg to 60kg dumbbells, barbells, and kettlebells.

Machines & Racks

State-of-the-art squat racks, bench presses, cable machines, and Smith machines.

Cardio Areas

Endurance. Energy. Elevation.

Our cardio areas are designed to keep your heart pumping and your energy high. Whether you're a morning treadmill runner or a late-night spin fanatic, our zone offers both comfort and intensity with equipment that adapts to your training level. With cityscape views, integrated media stations, and smart tracking technology, your cardio experience is both immersive and effective.

Treadmills & Stairmasters

Multiple incline, speed, and HIIT program options for all fitness levels. Shock-absorbing surfaces minimize joint strain.

Spin & Air Bikes

Perfect for high-intensity intervals or steady-state cardio, with adjustable resistance and ergonomic seating.

Rowing & Ellipticals

Low-impact options that boost cardiovascular health, balance, and full-body coordination.

Personal Trainers

Guided Progress. Personalised Results.

Our certified personal trainers are here to take your training to the next level. Whether your goal is fat loss, muscle gain, athletic performance, or rehabilitation, we match you with the right trainer to suit your journey. Expect personalized programs, expert technique correction, nutrition advice, and accountability from experienced professionals who care about your progress.

One-on-One Coaching

Get focused attention on your form, progress tracking, and goal-specific programming tailored to you.

Small Group Training

Train with a friend or in small groups of 3–5 with shared goals — keeping energy high and costs low.

Sports Performance

Speed, agility, and strength coaching for athletes preparing for competitions or improving physical performance.

🚀 Built for Tomorrow

Step into a next-gen fitness environment engineered for the future. Our facility features AI-powered pods that adapt workouts in real-time, ambient lighting that reacts to your energy output, and smart flooring that tracks motion and posture accuracy to reduce injury and enhance performance.

🧠 Smart Fitness

From biometric wearables to live performance dashboards, Virtus enables you to unlock your body's full potential. Receive instant insights into recovery, VO₂ max, and mobility while syncing your progress with your personalised Virtus training plan — accessible both in-house and on the go.

🤝 Community Culture

More than machines — we build meaningful connections. Whether you're attending a beginner workshop, recovery session, or elite competition training, you're part of a team that lifts each other up. Our inclusive events, wellness check-ins, and member socials keep you engaged and motivated.

🌐 Virtual Integration

Through VirtusConnect, bring the gym to your fingertips. Access remote live sessions, AI-personalised programs, and one-on-one coaching — all synchronised with your wearable data. It's a full hybrid training experience, whether you're at home, travelling, or in one of our partner gyms globally.
